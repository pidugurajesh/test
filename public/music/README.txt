==============================================================================
🎵 HOW TO ADD YOUR BACKGROUND MUSIC
==============================================================================

Simply place your favorite romantic MP3 song in this folder:
`public/music/background.mp3`

Requirements handled automatically by the app:
- Music only starts after Princess clicks the "OPEN YOUR LITTLE UNIVERSE" button.
- If no MP3 is provided yet, a soft dreamy romantic ambient chime plays gracefully using the Web Audio API!
- The floating music widget allows play, pause, mute, and volume adjustments at any time.
==============================================================================
